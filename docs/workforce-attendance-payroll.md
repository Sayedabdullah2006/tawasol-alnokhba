# Automatic attendance settlement

Pending release; requires `20261022213937_linked_work_sessions.sql`, then `20261022221900_automatic_attendance_payroll.sql` and matching application code. Do not migrate production for a preview.

## Rules approved for this release

- Settlement starts in October 2026, clipped to each employee's joining date. Management explicitly approved back-calculating October from the old attendance records.
- Old flexible days retain attendance after 17:00. `payroll_attendance_policy.cutoff_on` defaults to the first full Riyadh day after rollout. The new managed sessions also enforce their own 17:00 cutoff immediately.
- Historical flexible days close at midnight. New days close at 17:00 Riyadh. Neither an unfinished day nor future days create shortages.
- Each completed scheduled day is evaluated separately, with at most the configured daily attendance target credited for ordinary salary. Extra hours on a different day do not erase a shortage; raw attendance and the monthly progress card still preserve all genuinely recorded time.
- Friday/default nonwork days, approved leave, and company paid-calendar exceptions are exempt. Approved permissions merge with attendance to avoid double credit. Test-run requests are excluded.
- Founding Day (22 February) and National Day (23 September) are exempt. Variable Eid/other paid holidays must be populated in `wf_paid_calendar_days` by the company administrator/service before the affected dates. Attendance `daily_minutes` must reflect any reduced schedule applicable to that employee (including Ramadan).
- Hourly rate uses the full contractual monthly basic salary plus fixed allowances, divided by 30 and 8. The effective compensation version for each day is used. An already prorated joining-month statement is never prorated again. Incentives and insurance do not change the hourly rate.
- Missing seconds are multiplied by the unrounded hourly rate; round the month total once to halalas. Merge overlapping periods; preserve the paid 30-minute break and exclude excess break time using the shared attendance credit ranges.
- Amounts are computed and applied automatically without manager approval of the settlement. Existing overall payroll approval/payment recording remains in place.
- Manual insurance/other deductions and incentives remain separate. The automatic adjustment cannot make a net balance negative or reduce it below already recorded payments. Any unapplied excess is visible in the tooltip. Fully paid statements are frozen; historical paid corrections require a separate financial adjustment, not a rewrite of paid history.

## One authoritative ledger

The before-write trigger calculates `attendance_amount`, `attendance_details`, `deductions_amount`, and `total_amount`. Client-supplied totals cannot bypass it. `wf_attendance_payroll_snapshot` validates the actual actor and ownership, refreshes the statement, and returns the same totals used in the employee card, manager payroll editor, finance details, and history. Missing current/past monthly statements initialize silently from fixed salary terms and joining-date proration. Existing monthly basic/allowance snapshots are preserved.

`wf_refresh_attendance_payroll` runs each minute through pg_cron. Foreground cards refresh each minute and after attendance changes. Changes/corrections therefore update without a separate finance approval. Repeated unchanged refreshes do not increment the version or duplicate the audit trail. Payments recalculate under the payroll row lock before validating the payable balance.

Automatic calculation writes only the payroll row and `wf_payroll_attendance_audit`; it never creates a notification or mail job. Normal explicit payroll save/approve/payment notifications retain their existing behavior. No test globally disables real notifications.

Raw financial records remain owner/admin only. Task-management/review authority alone never grants financial access. A later explicit management instruction grants Noor read-only access to attendance/payroll card summaries through `can_view_team_summaries`; the scoped endpoint strips payment references, payroll notes, incentive references, requests and other HR fields. It grants no write, approval, correction or clock-in authority over colleagues. Sensitive RPCs are service-only, validate the actor, and use invoker privileges. Calendar exceptions expose only date/title.

## Overtime requests (pending release)

Apply `20261022232330_overtime_requests_payroll.sql` last. `overtime` is a real employee-request kind, appears in the request type selector and `/employee/requests/overtime` navigation, and uses the existing request history, attachments, RLS, review and notification flows. The employee selects one Riyadh work date and start/end times (midnight ending is supported). The manager can adjust the period during approval in one transaction; only administrators can approve. A pending, rejected or cancelled request contributes nothing to payroll.

Management-approved hourly formula: `(contractual actual monthly wage + 0.5 × contractual basic monthly wage) / 30 / 8`. Actual wage is the basic plus all fixed allowances in the compensation version effective on the work day. The request captures the source wages, exact duration, rate and approved payroll month. Amounts multiply source wages by exact seconds before one final rounding to cents; the client adjustment preview uses the same rational calculation with integer cents. Two hours: Noor66.92 SAR, Monwa46.67 SAR; Noor3 hours100.38 SAR.

The database validates positive same-day periods up to midnight, joining date, configured wages, and overlap with another pending/approved overtime request for the same employee. Ordinary 09:00–17:00 overlap is rejected on workdays; Fridays and paid holidays may be requested independently. Friday punch restrictions remain unchanged. Requests do not create ordinary attendance sessions or credit regular required hours.

`wf_monthly_payroll.overtime_amount` is derived from unique approved, non-test source requests; it is not copied into manual incentive items. A trigger recomputes base + manual incentive items + approved overtime, so saving a statement or refreshing it cannot add overtime twice. Approval refreshes/initializes the eligible payroll snapshot immediately, while future payroll months initialize when that month opens. Historical work is posted no earlier than the approval month. A fully paid current statement is preserved and overtime is assigned to the next month. The approved source request's financial fields cannot be mutated afterward. Existing salary approval/payment commands include generated overtime once in validation and totals.

The shared employee/Noor team-summary card shows a separate `خارج دوام` row. Salary-only totals exclude overtime and daily net includes it exactly once as an approved award. Overtime does not change the fixed insured wage. Employee/admin request decisions emit the standard data-change event so card refresh is immediate. The finance editor displays generated overtime without editable duplicate incentive rows.

Scoped test mode now permits overtime requests but retains all other restrictions; approved requests marked with `test_run_id` are excluded from real payroll posting and totals. ## Fixed annual paid holidays (pending release)

Apply `20261022233702_fixed_paid_holidays.sql` last. February22 (Founding Day) and September23 (National Day) are recurring Gregorian paid holidays, computed every year without annual seed jobs. Payroll/leave/OT calculations already exempt these fixed dates; this migration completes the punch guard, absence eligibility and weekly-report target integration. No synthetic attendance session or task time is created. Both check-in and check-out are rejected by authoritative Riyadh date, and the live bar marks the named paid holiday and disables punches. Record-scoped testing and normal real-event delivery behavior are unchanged.

Monthly attendance targets remove a scheduled working holiday's8 required hours; actual worked time remains actual. The attendance cards show paid official holiday hours separately, and the Excel summary adds a separate paid-holiday duration column. A holiday before joining or outside the reviewed month earns no hours; a holiday coinciding with Friday does not add duplicate attendance credit. These are the fixed dates requested, not an implementation of alternate-day or variable Eid calendars. Existing admin-managed paid-calendar exceptions are honored by salary/reminder/report SQL as before.

No missing-attendance reminder or manager escalation is eligible on either fixed holiday. Both fresh generation and stale-delivery eligibility use the same helper. The read-only weekly report excludes the holiday from required hours so its feedback does not classify the paid day as missing work. Paid holidays do not consume the annual leave bank.
