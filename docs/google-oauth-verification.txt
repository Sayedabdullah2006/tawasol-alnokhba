Scope justification (for Google's data-access review)
openid / email: Identify the Google organizer during explicit connection, verify
the email address, and restrict the company integration to its approved organizer.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned:
The application lets the company's authorized managers create, reschedule and
cancel Google Calendar events with a unique Google Meet conference, select
participants and associate the event with an internal task. The app reads an
event it created to recover its Meet link and synchronize updates idempotently.
Read-only access cannot create or change an event. Access to calendars owned by
other users is unnecessary, so we use events.owned instead of calendar.events or
the broader calendar scope. Operations target the organizer's primary calendar.

User flow
An administrator deliberately opens the Google connection settings. Google
authorization requests only openid, email and calendar.events.owned. The callback
checks the Google identity and required scope before storing an encrypted refresh
token. The company organizer is shared by authorized managers; employees do not
have to authorize personal calendars. Only invited/authorized users can view
their meetings. Inviting, updating and cancelling genuine meetings can notify
participants through Google. A separate app reminder links back to the site.

Data handling
We retain organizer email/Google subject, encrypted authorization data and the
meeting identifiers, Meet URLs, schedule, description and selected participants
needed for company meetings. Google data is not sold or used for advertising,
marketing or AI training. Users can revoke Google access through their account
connections and request deletion at support@nukhba.media. Revocation stops future
access; it does not automatically erase prior events or work records. The public
privacy policy explains storage, access, processors and deletion requests.
