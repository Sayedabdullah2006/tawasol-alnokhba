Customer notification identities

Logo/footer and duplicate protection update:
- FirstSaudi emails embed a PNG rasterization of the approved original logo using CID. It is carried inside the message, not fetched through a protected page. PDF invoice attachments remain separate and unchanged.
- Commercial boilerplate, the customer/interest explanation, and the redundant automatic-message footer are removed from both brands. Existing already-delivered messages cannot be rewritten.
- Order events use stable notification keys. Payment and request-received notifications have one event key per order; different review/offer/business content is a new event.
- customer_email_deliveries stores only digests, outcome, claim token, provider ID and timestamps; no addresses, bodies, codes or invoice data. The table and claim/finish RPCs are service-role-only.
- Concurrent attempts share an atomic claim and provider Idempotency-Key. A sent receipt suppresses repeats even when a template/logo changes. Unknown outcomes older than 23 hours require review instead of blind resend beyond the provider's idempotency window.
- Daily reminders and the central reminder helper use the same order/status/reminder-round key. Admin reminders preserve their retry key. A legitimately new notice or reminder round remains deliverable.
- FirstSaudi order integration currently sends no parallel order emails; its password-recovery mail is a separate account event. No FirstSaudi frontend/API change is needed for this update. Keep central order notifications as the sole sender for order lifecycle events.
- Validation: 74 template/source tests, 9 live isolated receipt/RPC checks and 5 transport/concurrency/lost-response checks. No new real customer/admin test email was sent for this update.
